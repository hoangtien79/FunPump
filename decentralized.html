Consensus Mechanism

FunCoin utilizes a Proof-of-Stake (PoS) consensus mechanism, which is more energy-efficient and secure than traditional Proof-of-Work systems. Validators are chosen based on their stake in the network, ensuring those with the most to lose have the most incentive to act honestly.
